Information We Collect

Canopy Global Pte. Ltd. ("Canopy", "we", "us") collects information you provide directly when you create an account, purchase carbon credits, or contact our team. This includes:

  • Full legal name and corporate entity name
  • Corporate email address and phone number
  • Billing address and payment method details (processed via PCI-DSS compliant gateways)
  • KYC documentation (passport, company registration) for institutional accounts
  • ESG reporting preferences and carbon offset goals

We also automatically collect technical data: IP address, browser type, device identifiers, pages visited, and interaction timestamps via server logs and session analytics.

2

How We Use Your Information

Your data is used exclusively to operate and improve the Canopy platform. Specifically, we use your information to:

  • Process carbon credit purchases and issue verified retirement certificates
  • Register credit transactions on the immutable Canopy blockchain registry
  • Generate ESG impact reports for your corporate sustainability disclosures
  • Send transactional emails, invoices, and audit notifications
  • Comply with anti-money laundering (AML) and know-your-customer (KYC) obligations
  • Improve our satellite verification platform through anonymized usage analytics
3

Data Sharing & Disclosure

Canopy does not sell or rent your personal data. We share data only with:

  • Registry Partners: Verra, Gold Standard, and other accreditation bodies require project and transaction data to issue credits
  • Third-Party Auditors: Anonymized project data is shared with verification bodies (South Pole, Bureau Veritas, TÜV Rheinland)
  • Payment Processors: Stripe and SWIFT for PCI-compliant transaction processing
  • Regulatory Authorities: When legally required under applicable financial crime or carbon market regulations
4

Carbon Credit Transactions

All carbon credit purchases are recorded on the Canopy blockchain registry and are permanently public as part of our immutable audit commitment. Your corporate name may appear on publicly accessible retirement certificates. If anonymization is required, please contact our enterprise team prior to completing a purchase.

Transaction IDs (e.g., CNP-992-B04-VER-24) are linked to your account and disclosed to the relevant carbon standard registry to prevent double-counting under the Paris Agreement Article 6 framework.
5

Cookies & Analytics

We use essential cookies for authentication and session management, and analytics cookies (anonymized) to improve platform performance. We do not use third-party advertising trackers. You may disable non-essential cookies via your browser without impacting core platform functionality.

  • Essential Cookies: Required for login session and security tokens
  • Analytics Cookies: Anonymized page performance data (no PII transmitted)
  • No Advertising Trackers: Canopy never loads Meta Pixel, Google Ads, or equivalent SDKs
6

Data Retention

Personal account data is retained for 7 years after account closure for regulatory compliance. Carbon transaction records are retained permanently as part of the immutable registry ledger. Anonymized analytics data is retained for 24 months.

  • Account personal data: 7 years post-closure
  • Carbon credit registry records: Permanent (immutable ledger requirement)
  • Anonymized analytics: 24 months rolling
  • KYC / AML documents: As required by applicable law (typically 5–7 years)
7

Your Rights (GDPR / PDPA)

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or export your personal data. To exercise these rights, contact our Data Protection Officer.

  • Right of Access: Request a copy of your personal data held by Canopy
  • Right of Rectification: Correct inaccurate or incomplete personal data
  • Right of Erasure: Request deletion of personal data (subject to legal retention obligations)
  • Right of Portability: Receive your data in a machine-readable format
  • Right to Object: Object to certain data processing activities
Note: Registry transaction records cannot be deleted due to the immutability requirements of the Verra VCS, Gold Standard, and ISO 14064 carbon credit frameworks.
